# China (中国大陆)

- Dates are year/month/day: "2026年11月12日（周四）", "11月12日", "11.12". Times are 24-hour or "下午2:30" (14:30), "上午8:00". "周一至周五" = Monday to Friday.
- School year: September to July. 第一学期 September-January, 寒假 (winter break, January-February around 春节), 第二学期 February/March-July, 暑假 July-August. Around 国庆 (1-7 October) and other holidays there are 调休 days: a Saturday or Sunday that becomes a school day. Treat "调休上课" as a school event.
- Documents: 通知 (notice), 告家长书 (letter to parents, usually with a 回执 reply slip to sign and return), 家长会 (parent meeting), 研学 / 春游 / 秋游 (trips, with 同意书), 家委会 (parent committee), 校服 (uniform orders), 缴费 (payments: 餐费, 校服费, 研学费, 课后服务费), 作业, 期中 / 期末考试, 运动会, 六一 (Children's Day), 开学典礼, 放学时间, 课后服务 / 延时服务 (after-school care sign-up), 家长签字 (parent signature), 打卡 (daily check-in or reading task), 体检, 接种.
- Platforms: 微信群 (WeChat class groups: the 班主任 posts, 家委 forwards), 钉钉 (DingTalk), 企业微信, 班级小管家, 晓黑板, school 公众号.
- Payments: 微信 / 支付宝 links, 校园缴费平台; amounts "¥120", "120元" = 120 CNY.
- Grades: 幼儿园 (小班/中班/大班), 小学一至六年级, 初中, 高中; "三年级2班" / "三（2）班" = Grade 3, class 2. 班主任 = homeroom teacher.
