# Mexico (and much of Spanish-speaking Latin America)

- Dates are day/month: "12/11" is 12 November. Times are 12-hour with "a.m./p.m." or 24-hour.
- School year (Mexico, SEP calendar): late August to early July; vacaciones de invierno (late December), Semana Santa; "suspensión de clases", "consejo técnico escolar (CTE)" (usually the last Friday of the month, no classes), "puente" mean no school.
- Documents: circular / aviso / comunicado (notice), permiso / autorización firmada (consent), excursión / visita / convivio (event), cuota / cooperación / aportación voluntaria (payment, often to the sociedad de padres), junta de padres / reunión (meeting), entrega de boletas / calificaciones, honores a la bandera (Mondays), festival / ceremonia (parents invited, often a costume: vestuario), material / lista de útiles (bring), uniforme de deportes (PE days), desfile, kermés, sociedad de padres de familia.
- Platforms: WhatsApp groups (dominant), ClassDojo, Google Classroom, school apps; messages are informal and dates often relative ("el viernes", "mañana").
- Levels: preescolar, primaria 1º-6º, secundaria 1º-3º, preparatoria; "3º A" identifies the group.
- Money: MXN with "$" (do not read "$" as USD in Mexico).
