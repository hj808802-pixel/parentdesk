# South Korea (대한민국)

- Dates are year/month/day: "2026.11.12.(수)" or "11월 12일(수)". The weekday in parentheses confirms the date. Times are 24-hour or "오후 3시" (15:00).
- School year: March to February; first semester March to July, second semester late August to December; winter break late December to February; 봄방학 is short, late February.
- Documents: 가정통신문 (letter home, often with a tear-off 회신서/동의서 at the bottom), 알림장 (daily class note, informal), 안내문, 신청서 (application form, a deadline), 동의서 (consent), 수익자부담경비 (parent-paid cost, a payment), 현장체험학습 (field trip: consent + payment), 수련회/수학여행 (overnight trips), 학부모 상담 주간 (conference week, book a slot), 공개수업 (open class), 운동회/학예회 (sports day / performance), 방과후학교 신청 (after-school program sign-up), 급식비/우유급식 (lunch/milk fees), 학교 안전 교육, 재량휴업일 (school-decided day off), 개교기념일.
- Platforms: e알리미, 하이클래스, 클래스팅, 아이엠스쿨, 학교 홈페이지 공지, 카카오톡 학부모 단톡방, 키즈노트 (kindergarten/daycare), 유치원 알림장.
- Payments: 스쿨뱅킹 (automatic school banking debit on a stated date) or 가상계좌; "납부" is a payment, "제출" is a hand-in.
- Grades: 초등 1~6학년, 중 1~3, 고 1~3; "3학년 2반" identifies the class.
- Money: KRW with "원". Write amounts as numbers, currency KRW.
