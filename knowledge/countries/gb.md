# United Kingdom (and Ireland)

- Dates are day/month; "12/11" is 12 November. Times are often 24-hour ("15:15") or "3.15pm".
- School year: early September to late July, three terms with half-term breaks (late October, mid-February, late May); INSET days (teacher training, school closed) are all-day events.
- Common items: "letter home" (notice), consent form or reply slip (consent), trip contribution (payment, via ParentPay, SchoolMoney, Arbor, ScoPay or ParentMail), non-uniform day / own clothes day (often with a donation), World Book Day (costume), school photos, parents' evening (book a slot online), PE kit days, forest school kit, show-and-tell, reading record, homework due, Year 6 residential, nativity or assembly (parents invited), after-school club sign-up, breakfast club.
- Platforms: ParentMail, ParentPay, Arbor, ClassDojo, Tapestry (early years), Seesaw, Class Charts, MCAS (My Child At School), Weduc.
- Year groups: Reception, Year 1-13; "KS1 / KS2" are key stages. Ireland: Junior Infants to 6th Class, then 1st-6th Year; "mid-term break" and "voluntary contribution" are common.
- Money: GBP with "£" (EUR in Ireland).
