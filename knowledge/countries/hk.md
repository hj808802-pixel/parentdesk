# Hong Kong (香港)

- Dates are day/month/year: "12/11/2026", "2026年11月12日（星期四）". Notices are often bilingual (Chinese and English). Times are "下午2:30" or "2:30 p.m.".
- School year: September to July. 上學期 / 下學期; Christmas, Chinese New Year and Easter breaks; 考試 / 測驗 timetables; 統一測驗.
- Documents: 家長通告 / 通告 (circular, numbered "通告第12號", with a 回條 reply slip due by a date), 家長日 (parent day), 家長教師會 PTA, 校外參觀 / 戶外學習日 (outing with consent), 旅行日 (school picnic day), 運動會, 便服日 (casual-dress day with a donation), 繳費 (fees: 課外活動費, 旅行費, 書簿費, 午膳費), 功課, 默書 (dictation dates), 測驗, 考試, 課外活動 ECA, 校服 (summer/winter uniform change dates), 通告回條.
- Platforms: eClass / eClass Parent App, GRWTH, Tung Sing, school website, WhatsApp parent groups.
- Payments: AlipayHK, PPS, eClass ePayment, 八達通 (Octopus), cheque; amounts "HK$120", "$120", "港幣120元" = 120 HKD.
- Grades: K1-K3 (幼稚園), P1-P6 (小一至小六), S1-S6 (中一至中六); "3B班" / "小三B班". 班主任 = class teacher.
