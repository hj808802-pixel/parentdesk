# Canada

- Dates are usually month/day in English-speaking provinces and day/month in Quebec and in bilingual forms; follow the family's date order setting and quote the text you read.
- School year: early September to late June; winter break late December; March break (one week in March); PA / PD days (no school) scattered through the year.
- Common items: field trip form (consent) with a fee (payment, often through School Cash Online or SchoolCashOnline), pizza day and hot lunch orders (payment with an order deadline), picture day, Terry Fox run, spirit days, report cards, parent-teacher interviews, agenda signing, library day, gym day (indoor shoes).
- Platforms: School Cash Online, Edsby, SchoolMessenger, Seesaw, ClassDojo, Remind, Google Classroom.
- Money: CAD with "$".
- Quebec: "mot aux parents", "autorisation", "sortie éducative", "service de garde" (after-school care) are the usual words.
