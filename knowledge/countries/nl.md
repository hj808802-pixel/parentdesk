# Netherlands (Nederland)

- Dates are day/month/year: "12-11-2026", "donderdag 12 november". Times are 24-hour with a dot: "14.30 uur", "van 8.30 tot 14.00 uur".
- School year: late August or early September to July. Holiday weeks differ by region (Noord/Midden/Zuid): herfstvakantie (October), kerstvakantie, voorjaarsvakantie (February), meivakantie, zomervakantie (6 weeks). Studiedag = teacher training day, children free.
- Documents: nieuwsbrief, ouderbrief, schoolgids, toestemmingsformulier (consent), oudergesprek / 10-minutengesprek / kennismakingsgesprek (parent talk, booked in the app), rapport, Cito-toets, schoolreisje, schoolkamp, Sinterklaas (5 December, often a surprise/schoen), kerstviering, Koningsspelen (April), luizencontrole, gymkleding (PE kit on gym days), overblijven/TSO (lunch supervision), BSO (after-school care), continurooster, ouderbijdrage (voluntary parental contribution, a payment), sponsorloop, musical groep 8, schoolfotograaf.
- Platforms: Parro, Social Schools, Klasbord, SchouderCom, Kwieb, e-mail, "de schoolapp".
- Payments: via Schoolkassa, WIS Collect or an iDEAL link, "betalen vóór". Amounts "€ 25,-" or "€ 25,00" = 25 EUR.
- Grades: groep 1-8 in primary school ("groep 5"), then brugklas and leerjaar 1-6 in secondary school.
