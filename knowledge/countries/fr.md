# France (also Belgium)

- Dates are day/month: "12/11" is 12 November. Times are 24-hour ("15h30" = 15:30).
- School year: early September to early July; vacances de la Toussaint, de Noël, d'hiver (February, by zone A/B/C), de printemps (April, by zone), d'été. "Pont" days and "journée pédagogique" (no class) are all-day events.
- Documents: mot dans le cahier de liaison / carnet de correspondance (a note to sign: consent or info), autorisation parentale / autorisation de sortie (consent), sortie scolaire / classe découverte / voyage scolaire (trips: consent + payment), réunion de rentrée / réunion parents-professeurs (meeting), rendez-vous avec l'enseignant (choose a slot), coopérative scolaire / participation (payment), cantine / garderie / étude / périscolaire inscriptions (sign-ups with deadlines), photo de classe, fête de l'école / kermesse, spectacle, piscine (bring maillot, bonnet, serviette), tenue de sport, livret scolaire / bulletin, absence de l'enseignant.
- Platforms: Pronote, ENT (Éducation nationale), Klassroom, Educartable, Beneylu School, ONE, WhatsApp parent groups; Belgium: Smartschool, Konecto.
- Classes: maternelle (PS, MS, GS), CP, CE1, CE2, CM1, CM2, collège 6e-3e, lycée 2nde-Terminale.
- Money: EUR with "€"; "avant le" and "au plus tard le" mark deadlines; "chèque à l'ordre de" is a payment.
