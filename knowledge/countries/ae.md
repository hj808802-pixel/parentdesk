# United Arab Emirates

- Dates are day/month/year: "12/11/2026", "Thursday 12 November". Times are 12-hour: "2:30 pm". The school week is Monday to Friday with an early finish on Friday; shorter hours during Ramadan.
- School year: late August/September to late June/July. Term 1 September-December, Term 2 January-March, Term 3 April-June; winter break (December-January) and spring break (March/April). Public holidays: Eid (dates vary each year), National Day 2-3 December, Flag Day 3 November.
- Mostly international schools (British, American, IB, Indian, French curricula): circular, newsletter, consent form / permission slip, parent-teacher conference, Dress-up Day, World Book Day, UAE National Day celebrations (kandura/abaya or UAE colours), Flag Day, ECA (extra-curricular activities) sign-up with deadlines, school bus registration, canteen / lunch orders, uniform (summer/winter), Sports Day, INSET / PD day (no school), early finish, trips with a cost. Regulators: KHDA (Dubai), ADEK (Abu Dhabi), SPEA (Sharjah). Notices may also be in Arabic or Hindi.
- Platforms: school apps and portals (iSAMS, Engage, Toddle, ClassDojo, Seesaw, Edmodo, Google Classroom), e-mail, WhatsApp parent groups.
- Payments: online parent portal, bank transfer, cheque; amounts "AED 150", "150 AED", "Dhs 150" = 150 AED.
- Grades: FS1/FS2 and Year 1-13 (British), KG1/KG2 and Grade 1-12 (American/Indian); "Year 3 Blue", "Grade 3B".
