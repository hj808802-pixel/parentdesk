# Sweden (Sverige)

- Dates are year/month/day ("2026-11-12") or day/month ("12/11", "torsdag 12 november"). Times are 24-hour: "kl. 14.30". Week numbers are used constantly ("v. 46", "vecka 46"): convert week number plus weekday to a date (ISO weeks, Monday first).
- School year: late August to mid-June. Höstlov (week 44), jullov (late December-early January), sportlov (week 7-10 by region), påsklov, sommarlov. Studiedag = staff day, no lessons (fritids often open). Lovdag = day off.
- Documents: veckobrev (weekly letter, the main source), informationsbrev, utvecklingssamtal (development talk, book a time), föräldramöte (parent meeting), friluftsdag (outdoor day: packed lunch, clothes for weather), skolfotografering, Lucia (13 December, white clothes), skolavslutning (end-of-term ceremony), klassresa, simundervisning (swimming lessons), idrott (PE days, bring kläder), läxa (homework), prov, nationella prov, matsedel, fritids (after-school care), lovanmälan (sign up for holiday care), ledighetsansökan (leave request).
- Platforms: Schoolsoft, Unikum, Vklass, InfoMentor, Tyra (preschool), Edlevo, e-mail; parents log in with BankID.
- Payments: rare (school is free); class trips collected via Swish; amounts "150 kr" = 150 SEK.
- Grades: förskola, förskoleklass (F-klass), årskurs 1-9 ("åk 3", "klass 3B"), gymnasiet. Mentor = class teacher.
