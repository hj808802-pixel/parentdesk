# United States

- Dates are month/day; "11/12" is November 12. Times are 12-hour with AM/PM; "dismissal at 3:05" means 15:05.
- School year: mid-August or early September to late May or June; semesters or trimesters; winter break late December, spring break March or April; Thanksgiving break the last Thursday and Friday of November.
- Common items: permission slip (consent), field trip fee (payment, usually cash, check or an online portal such as MySchoolBucks, PayPal, Square), picture day and retakes, book fair, Scholastic book orders, spirit week dress-up days (one event per day), early dismissal / half day, late start, snow day or e-learning day, PTA/PTO meetings, fundraiser deadlines, report cards, conferences (sign up via SignUpGenius or the school portal), lunch account balance, yearbook orders.
- Platforms: ParentSquare, ClassDojo, Remind, Seesaw, Bloomz, PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Schoology, Google Classroom. Their notifications are terse; the body usually holds one item.
- Grades: K, 1st-12th; "Room 12" or a teacher's name identifies the class.
- Money: USD with "$"; checks payable to the school or PTA; "exact change" is common.
