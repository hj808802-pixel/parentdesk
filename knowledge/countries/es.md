# Spain

- Dates are day/month: "12/11" is 12 November. Times are 24-hour ("15:30") or "a las 3 y media".
- School year: early or mid-September to late June; Navidad break, Semana Santa, and regional holidays; "día no lectivo" and "puente" mean no school; "jornada continua" (June and September) means a shorter day.
- Documents: circular (notice), autorización (consent, often a tear-off "devolver firmada antes del"), excursión / salida / visita (trip: consent + payment), reunión de padres / tutoría (meeting or a slot to book), cuota / pago / ingreso en cuenta (payment, often a bank transfer with a concepto), comedor (lunch service sign-up), extraescolares (after-school activities with sign-up deadlines), material escolar, uniforme / chándal (PE kit days), foto escolar, fiesta de fin de curso, festival de Navidad, AMPA / AFA (parents' association), boletín de notas.
- Platforms: Dinantia, TokApp, ClassDojo, Alexia, Clickedu, Educamos, WhatsApp parent groups.
- Levels: Infantil (3-5 años), Primaria 1º-6º, ESO 1º-4º, Bachillerato; "3º B" identifies the class.
- Money: EUR with "€".
