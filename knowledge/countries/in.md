# India

- Dates are day/month/year: "12/11/2026", "12-11-26", "12th Nov". Times are 12-hour with am/pm ("2:30 pm sharp"). A weekday in brackets confirms the date.
- School year: April to March for most CBSE/ICSE/state-board schools (some southern states June to April). Summer break May-June; Term 1 April-September, Term 2 October-March; exams called Periodic Tests, Half-Yearly, Pre-Boards, Annual/Final.
- The standard word for a notice is "circular" (often numbered). Also: diary/almanac note, consent form, undertaking, PTM (parent-teacher meeting), fee circular, holiday circular, Annual Day, Sports Day, Investiture Ceremony, Children's Day (14 Nov), Teachers' Day (5 Sep), Republic Day (26 Jan) and Independence Day (15 Aug) celebrations with dress codes ("tricolour", "ethnic wear"), fancy dress, colour-dress day (no uniform), half day, picnic/excursion, Olympiad registration, activity fee, book list, summer camp.
- Platforms: school apps (Teno, Edunext, Campus Care, MyClassboard, MCB, ParentApp, Entab), ERP portals, SMS, e-mail, and WhatsApp class groups where a class representative or "class mom" forwards the teacher's message.
- Payments: "fee to be paid by", quarterly or term fees, online fee portal, UPI/NEFT, late fee after the due date. Amounts in ₹/Rs./INR; Indian digit grouping ("1,25,000" = 125000).
- Grades: Nursery, LKG, UKG, then Class 1-12 ("Class III-B", "Std 3", "Grade 3"), sections A/B/C.
- Register: "kindly", "requested to", "intimate", "revert", "ma'am/sir", "wards" (= children). Notices may be in Hindi or a regional language with English dates.
