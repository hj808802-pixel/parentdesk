# Philippines (Pilipinas)

- Dates are month/day/year: "11/12/2026", "November 12, 2026", "Nov. 12 (Thursday)". Times are 12-hour: "2:00 PM".
- School year: DepEd public schools open in June/July and end in March/April (the exact calendar shifts year to year); private schools similar. Quarters Q1-Q4, Christmas break (late December-early January), summer break April-May.
- Documents: memo, advisory, circular, letter to parents, parental consent / waiver (field trips, outbound), PTC (Parent-Teacher Conference), Homeroom PTA / GPTA meeting, card giving / recognition day / moving-up, Buwan ng Wika (August, Filipiniana attire), United Nations Day (October, costumes), Foundation Day, Intramurals, Christmas party (exchange gift with a budget), Brigada Eskwela (school clean-up before opening), modules, periodical test schedule, project submission, contribution/collection (class fees), GC (group chat).
- Platforms: Facebook Messenger group chats (the main channel: teacher and class officers post), Facebook pages, school apps and LMS, SMS.
- Messages mix English and Tagalog/Taglish: "Magandang araw po", "paki-send po", "bukas" (tomorrow), "sa Lunes" (on Monday), "dalhin" (bring), "bayad" (payment), "pasahan" (deadline), "po/opo" (polite particles).
- Payments: GCash, Maya, bank; amounts "₱150", "Php 150", "P150" = PHP.
- Grades: Kinder, Grade 1-6 (elementary), Grade 7-10 (junior high), Grade 11-12 (senior high); sections have names ("Grade 3 - Sampaguita").
