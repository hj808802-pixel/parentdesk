# Taiwan (台灣)

- Dates are year/month/day and often use the ROC calendar: "115年11月12日" = 2026-11-12 (ROC year + 1911 = Gregorian year); also "11/12（四）". Times are "下午2:30" (14:30) or 24-hour.
- School year: late August/September to June. 第一學期 September-January, 寒假 (January-February around 農曆新年), 第二學期 February-June, 暑假 July-August. 彈性放假 / 補班補課 days move school days around holidays: a Saturday 補課 is a school day.
- Documents: 通知單 / 家長通知書 (notice, usually with a 回條 reply slip to sign by a date), 聯絡簿 (daily contact book: teacher notes, parents sign), 親師座談會 / 班親會 (parent-teacher meeting), 校外教學 (field trip, with 同意書), 運動會, 園遊會, 校慶, 作業, 段考 / 期中考 / 期末考, 繳費 (午餐費, 班費, 課後照顧, 社團費), 課後社團, 制服 / 運動服 (uniform days), 導師 (homeroom teacher), 晨光時間, 戶外教育, 畢業旅行.
- Platforms: LINE 班級群組 (the main channel), school apps (校園通, ClassDojo, 親師生平台), the paper 聯絡簿.
- Payments: 超商代碼 (convenience-store payment), 轉帳, school 繳費單; amounts "NT$500", "500元" = 500 TWD.
- Grades: 幼兒園, 國小一至六年級, 國中, 高中; "三年二班" = Grade 3, class 2.
