# Indonesia

- Dates are day/month/year: "12/11/2026", "Kamis, 12 November 2026". Times are 24-hour with a dot: "pukul 07.30", "jam 14.00 WIB" (time zones WIB/WITA/WIT), or informal "jam 2 siang".
- School year: July to June. Semester 1 July-December, Semester 2 January-June; libur semester (December-January, June-July); Ramadan and Lebaran (Idul Fitri) holidays move each year; PTS/PAS (mid- and end-of-semester exams), ulangan harian.
- Documents: surat pemberitahuan / surat edaran (notice), undangan (invitation, e.g. rapat wali murid = parent meeting), surat izin (permission), formulir persetujuan (consent), study tour / outing / outbound / field trip, pengambilan rapor (report-card pick-up by parents), parenting (parent seminar), market day, class meeting, ekskul (extracurricular), seragam (uniform schedule: batik, pramuka, olahraga on set days), iuran / SPP / uang kegiatan (fees), infaq, pentas seni, perpisahan, MPLS (orientation), literasi, tugas / PR (homework).
- Platforms: WhatsApp groups ("grup wali murid"; the wali kelas posts, a koordinator/korlas forwards), school apps, Instagram, Google Classroom.
- Payments: bank transfer, virtual account, cash to the korlas; amounts "Rp 150.000" / "Rp150.000,-" = 150000 IDR (dot thousands).
- Grades: PAUD, TK A/B, SD kelas 1-6, SMP 7-9, SMA/SMK 10-12; "kelas 3B". Wali kelas = homeroom teacher; wali murid = parents/guardians; ananda = the child.
