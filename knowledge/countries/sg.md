# Singapore

- Dates are day/month/year: "12 Nov 2026", "12/11". Times are 12-hour ("2.30pm", "7.30am") or 24-hour ("1430"). School starts around 7.30am.
- School year: January to November. Term 1 January-March, Term 2 late March-May, Term 3 late June/July-September, Term 4 September-November; June holidays and November-December year-end holidays. PSLE (Primary 6) in late September/October; WA (weighted assessments), SA1/SA2 or year-end exams.
- Documents: letter to parents, circular, consent form / reply slip (often done in the app), Parent-Teacher Meeting (PTM), Meet-the-Parents session, CCA (co-curricular activity) notices, Learning Journey (field trip), Speech Day, Sports Day, Racial Harmony Day (21 July, ethnic costume), Children's Day (first Friday of October), Teachers' Day (early September), National Day (9 August; red/white attire), Total Defence Day (15 February), booklist, PE attire, SLS (Student Learning Space) homework, Mother Tongue, supplementary lessons, remedial.
- Platforms: Parents Gateway (PG, the MOE app: notices, consent, payment), ClassDojo, e-mail, WhatsApp parent support groups.
- Payments: Parents Gateway payment, GIRO, PayNow. Amounts "S$12" / "$12" = SGD.
- Grades: Primary 1-6 (P1-P6), Secondary 1-5 (Sec 1-5), JC1-2; classes named ("Class 3 Diligence", "3A").
