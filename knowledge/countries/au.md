# Australia (and New Zealand)

- Dates are day/month; "12/11" is 12 November. Times are 12-hour with am/pm.
- School year: late January or early February to mid-December, four terms with two-week holidays between; "pupil-free day" or "student-free day" means school closed. New Zealand: four terms from early February.
- Common items: "note" (notice), permission note or excursion note (consent), excursion / incursion (event), payment via Compass, Sentral, Qkr!, Flexischools, Xplor or Hero; canteen orders (payment with a cut-off day), book club (Scholastic), athletics / swimming carnival (bring hat, sunscreen, togs), Book Week parade (costume), Harmony Day, assembly, parent-teacher interviews, uniform shop, sports uniform days, sun-safe hat rules, "bring a plate" for events.
- Platforms: Compass, Sentral, Seesaw, Class Dojo, Skoolbag, Hero (NZ), Kindo (NZ).
- Year levels: Prep / Kindergarten / Foundation, Year 1-12.
- Money: AUD or NZD with "$".
